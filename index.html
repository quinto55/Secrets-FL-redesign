The house rules, in short

  • 21 and over. A photo ID is required at the door.
  • Members only. Every guest must be a member. Memberships are approved on site after an ID check and a short interview.
  • Consent, always. Zero tolerance for inappropriate behavior. Tell an attendant or press an emergency button.
  • No cameras in play areas. No photos or video in the courtyard or pool area.
  • Clothing optional in the courtyard. Cover up in the lobby and its restrooms, the hallways, café, nightclub and parking lot.
  • Single gentlemen. Limited to 10 per event, Wednesday to Monday, with passes sold until midnight. Except on Tuesdays, single men enter the playroom only with the couple who invited them.
  • Club dress code. No do-rags, ball caps, baggy clothing, tank tops or sportswear. Theme nights are the exception.
  • Discretion. What happens here stays here.
